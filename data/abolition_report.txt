ABOLITION YEAR
   best estimate: Nasi' year 19.3 AH  (1 Muharram 19 AH = approx 640-01-02 CE)
   range across samples: 17-21 AH (~638-641 CE)

   For reference, using 37/100 instead of the true 7/19 -- a 0.4% rate
   error -- would have given 24.6 AH. That is how sharp the leverage is.

SENSITIVITY
   dA/dr = K/r^2 = 3956 years per unit rate
   -> a 1% error in the intercalation rate moves A by 14.6 years
   an epoch misalignment of +-1 lunation moves A by 2.7 years

ASSUMPTIONS (both load-bearing)
   1. Year 1 of this calendar and year 1 AH are the same lunation.
   2. The intercalation rate has been constant since abolition.
