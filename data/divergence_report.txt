SANITY CHECK -- tabular Hijri for a few known dates
   2000-01-01: official Hijri 24/09/1420   |   Nasi' calendar 24 ذو القعدة 1378
   2026-08-20: official Hijri  6/03/1448   |   Nasi' calendar  7 شعبان 1405
   2026-10-12: official Hijri 29/04/1448   |   Nasi' calendar  1 رمضان 1405
   2100-12-31: official Hijri 29/10/1524   |   Nasi' calendar  1 ذو الحجة 1479

1. YEAR-NUMBER GAP (official Hijri year minus Nasi' year), measured
   2000 CE: official 1421  ours 1379  gap 42
   2001 CE: official 1422  ours 1380  gap 42
   2002 CE: official 1423  ours 1381  gap 42
   2050 CE: official 1472  ours 1429  gap 43
   2051 CE: official 1473  ours 1430  gap 43
   2098 CE: official 1522  ours 1477  gap 45
   2099 CE: official 1523  ours 1478  gap 45
   2100 CE: official 1524  ours 1479  gap 45
   gap grew 42 -> 45 (= 3) across 100 Nasi' years
   measured rate: 0.030000 extra official-years per Nasi' year
   theoretical rate from 37 insertions / 101 yrs: 0.030528

2. EXTRAPOLATING THE GAP BACK TO ZERO
   using measured rate (0.030000):
     1500.0 Nasi' years before 2100 CE
     -> separation at Nasi' year -21.0, i.e. about 600 CE
   using theoretical rate (0.030528):
     1474.1 Nasi' years before 2100 CE
     -> separation at Nasi' year +4.9, i.e. about 626 CE

3. WHAT THAT MEANS
   Both calendars are anchored to the same epoch (year 1 = 622 CE):
     official Hijri year 1 -> 622 CE (lunar years, ~354.37 d)
     Nasi' year 1          -> 622 CE (solar-locked years, ~365.29 d)
   They share an epoch but count differently, so the year NUMBERS
   separate essentially at the epoch itself -- not centuries later.

CAVEAT -- this is extrapolation, not data.
   The source table covers 2000-2100 CE only. Everything above assumes the
   intercalation rate held steady for ~1400 years. The book states it built
   the calendar from 513 CE, and describes longer correction cycles, so the
   real historical rate is very unlikely to be exactly constant.
   The book's own 513-2000 charts would answer this from data; they are in
   neither PDF supplied here.
